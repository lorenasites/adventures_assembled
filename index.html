Meet Your Trusted Advisor

"I don't just book trips;
I assemble legacies."

Hi, I’m Tara Lennox, owner and Adventure Creator behind Adventures Assembled Travel Co.

I’ve been helping clients turn travel dreams into thoughtfully planned vacations since 2013, and in 2020 I launched Adventures Assembled with a simple belief: planning an incredible vacation should feel exciting, not overwhelming.

Today, I specialize in creating personalized travel experiences for couples, families, groups, and multigenerational travelers around the world. From ocean and river cruises to European adventures, luxury escapes, Disney and Universal vacations, Alaska, honeymoons, and bucket-list journeys, every trip I design is built around the client’s needs.

Travel is something I take personally. I’ve sailed dozens of cruises across a wide range of cruise lines, traveled extensively throughout Europe, the Caribbean, South and North America and beyond. I continually invest in firsthand experiences, industry training, and relationships with my travel partners and tour operators. That experience allows me to go far beyond simply booking a hotel or cruise cabin. I help my clients navigate the details, compare the right options, anticipate potential challenges, and create an experience that truly fits the way they want to travel.

I’ve been fortunate to be recognized as a Top 150 Advisor out of more than 12,000 travel advisors within my consortia for five consecutive years. I’m also proud to be part Opulence, an exclusive luxury travel division for advisors specializing in elevated travel experiences. For me, these opportunities are less about the titles and more about the relationships, resources, and industry connections they allow me to bring to my clients.

Before entering the travel industry, I earned a master’s degree in counseling and worked in mental health and education. That background has shaped the way I approach travel planning today. I listen closely, ask thoughtful questions, and pay attention to the little details that help me better understand what each client truly wants from their vacation.

I’m also a wife, mom of two boys, longtime military spouse, and lifelong believer that some of the best memories we create happen when we step outside our everyday routines and experience the world together.

Whether you’re planning a milestone trip, a family adventure, your first cruise, or the vacation you’ve been dreaming about for years, my goal is to make the entire experience feel easier, exceptionally well cared for, and personal.
